North India Company website

Upload everything in this folder to your web host (Hostinger, Netlify, GoDaddy, etc.). index.html is the home page.

Retailer links: open site.js and paste each listing URL into STORE_LINKS / PRODUCT_LINKS at the top. Empty = 'opening soon'.
Hero video: save your Gemini video as images/hero.mp4 and it plays on the home page automatically.
Before launch: replace [Legal entity name], [Registered business address] and [State or province] in privacy.html and terms.html, and check the domain in sitemap.xml and robots.txt.
